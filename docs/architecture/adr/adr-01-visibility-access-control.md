# ADR-01: Audience Visibility and Access-Control Policy

- Status: draft
- Owner: Salama
- Date: TBD

## Context
Who can see a post, who can restrict its audience, and what visibility levels does the
platform need? Team agreed (2026-09-29): campus moderators can hide/remove any post; group
officers can additionally remove posts within their own group. Appeals process still open.

## Alternatives considered
1. Moderators only (centralized, slower, consistent)
2. Moderators + group officers for their own group (chosen — faster response, some inconsistency risk)
3. Fully decentralized (any group member can remove) — rejected, too easy to abuse

## Decision
Moderators + group officers for their own group. Salama to write up the full rationale,
audience visibility levels (public campus / group-only / private), and appeals process.
